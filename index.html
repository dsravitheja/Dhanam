Important Notes & Caveats
  • Perquisite per the Income-tax Rules, 2026 (in force 1 Apr 2026): ≤1600cc or electric = ₹5,000/mo · >1600cc = ₹7,000/mo · company driver = +₹3,000/mo. These replaced the long-standing ₹1,800/₹2,400/₹900 figures under the Income-tax Rules, 1962 — roughly a 3× increase, so a car package that looked worthwhile under the old table may not under this one.
  • Old regime includes standard deduction (₹50,000) + 80C (₹1.5L) only. HRA, 80D excluded for simplicity.
  • New regime (FY2025-26): standard deduction ₹75,000; rebate u/s 87A for taxable ≤ ₹12L, with marginal relief up to ≈₹12.71L.
  • EPF is taken from the amount you enter, not derived from salary — real EPF applies to Basic + DA only, which this app has no way to infer from total fixed pay. The same figure is used in all three scenarios, since a car carve-out comes out of allowances rather than Basic.
  • Carve-out (A): your CTC reduces by car package; employer pays EMI/fuel/driver. Your take-home reduces but no car expense.
  • Additive (B): car is on top of your CTC; full salary retained; only perquisite value added to taxable income.
  • Consult a CA for personalised tax planning before restructuring your salary.

Important Notes & Caveats
  • Prices, names and mileage/efficiency are exactly what you typed — nothing here is verified against a dealer quote or a catalog.
  • The resale credit uses the IRDAI insurance depreciation schedule (20% year 1, 15%/yr after) as a proxy for what the car is worth after the lease — that's an insurance schedule, not a resale-price prediction, and it's one ICE-shaped curve applied to EVs too (battery health and faster model turnover aren't modelled separately).
  • Ranking and the gold "cheapest" highlight use net cost before the resale credit — the after-resale figure is shown on every card, it just doesn't drive the rank.
  • Insurance is one flat rate per vehicle type (ICE vs EV), not a per-model quote.
  • The city:highway driving split and its multipliers are global — every car is assumed driven the same way, with no per-car override.
  • Unlike the spreadsheet this section is based on, there's no "EV driven more because it feels free" multiplier — annual km is the same figure for every car, so the ₹/km figures stay comparable across ICE and EV.
  • Perquisite per the Income-tax Rules, 2026 (in force 1 Apr 2026): ≤1600cc or electric = ₹5,000/mo · >1600cc = ₹7,000/mo · company driver = +₹3,000/mo — the same table the lease analysis above uses.
  • Marginal tax rate is a single number you enter here, not derived from a slab calculation — it doesn't pick up the Section 87A relief the salary panel above applies.
  • Consult a CA and get dealer quotes before acting on this comparison.
